Grades & Sections

This page is the school's class list. Every grade and section a learner signs up under, every section a material is posted to and every class a teacher can open comes from here, so a section can never grow a new spelling on its own. Renaming one moves its learners, scores and materials with it; a class that still holds learners cannot be removed until they are moved on the Assessment page.

Schedule & time is where each class meets: tick the days it runs, type when it starts and ends, say which Room it meets in, then press Save schedule. A class with double periods can meet at more than one time in the week - press + Add time slot and fill each slot with its own days and times; two slots on the same day may touch back to back but never overlap. Leave a slot empty to drop it, and clear them all to take the schedule away. The room is the one box for the whole week: a class that moves room between slots is written as two classes. This is the section's own time; every teacher listed beside it can be given their own days, time and room under their name, and that one is what shows on their profile.

Assign a teacher to a class

A teacher opens the records of the classes listed here under their name and nothing else. They may keep as many classes as the school gives them: tick every section in the dropdown and one submit hands them all the same subjects and schedule - no need to repeat the form per class. Tick the subjects they teach in those classes: their quiz and test pickers then offer only those subjects. Leave them all off and the teacher keeps the whole grade to choose from. Each teacher also gets their own days and time here, plotted on a weekly table on their profile; use Set schedule under a teacher in the list above to change it later. If two of a teacher's classes meet at the same time, the save message warns you and the profile shows that cell in red.

No-class days (school-wide)

When the whole school is closed — no classes, it is a non-working holiday — write the date and the note here. On that day the class-start reminders and the morning Today’s classes digest are halted for everyone, and the note shows on the learner and staff profiles in place of the day’s classes. Only the main admin writes these; every admin can read them.
