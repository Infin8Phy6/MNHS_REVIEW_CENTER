How it scores

  • One attempt per item — on the standard level a pressed choice answers it for you.
  • The level is fixed when the activity starts and is printed on the result sheet.
  • On the hard level the capitalisation is part of the answer: right letters in the wrong case are marked wrong, and the feedback tells you when that happened.
  • 1 point per item, {{ total }} points in total, and the answer is marked on this computer.
  • After every try the completed element tile appears with its atomic number, symbol, name, atomic mass, electron configuration, oxidation states, state of matter and metal class.
  • Download your result sheet as a PNG for the teacher.
  • This sprint is practice: its score is not filed under your Review Center results, which only keep quiz attempts.
