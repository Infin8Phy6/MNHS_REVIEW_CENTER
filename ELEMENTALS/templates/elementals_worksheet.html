Prepare a Symbol Sprint activity sheet

The button draws the tiles at random, exactly like a game round, and opens a printable activity sheet: twenty tiles on one A4 page — two columns of ten — each tile carrying the clues and its own answer space for the symbol. The sheet page also prints with answers for the teacher, and the same two versions download as PDFs.
