How the round scores

  • The free clue is always the letters of the name, shuffled. They are dealt again every ten seconds, or press Shuffle the letters yourself.
  • Type the whole name in the answer box, then press Submit answer.
  • Give it with no clue shown and the person is worth {{ start_points }} points.
  • Reveal Clue 1 and it becomes {{ start_points - 1 }} points, Clue 2 {{ start_points - 2 }}, Clue 3 {{ start_points - 3 }}.
  • Once the third clue is shown there is a {{ clue_seconds }} second count, then the round moves to the next person.
  • A person you cannot answer still leaves you with 1 point, so nobody loses everything.
  • Everything is added up at the end of the round — there is no limit to how many people one activity holds.
